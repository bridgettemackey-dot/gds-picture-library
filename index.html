Take a picture, share it anywhere

Artwork from six Bahamian children's books. All of it is free to download and free to repost — on WhatsApp, on Instagram, in a classroom newsletter, anywhere you like.

Every picture carries a code. Underneath each one is a strip with the book's name and a square code. Anyone who scans that code goes straight to the book on Amazon. That is how a picture keeps working after you have shared it, so please leave the strip on.

GDS Publications is the publishing arm of The GDS Group of Companies, Nassau, The Bahamas. Everything here is illustration or artwork from the books. There are no photographs of children on this site.
